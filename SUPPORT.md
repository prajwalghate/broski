# Getting help

## Before you ask

- [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md) covers the mic, recognition, shortcuts, models, sync and opening the app.
- [Getting started](docs/getting-started.md), [Choosing your AI](docs/models.md) and [The iPhone app](docs/iphone.md) answer most setup questions.

## Where to ask

- **Questions and ideas:** [Discussions](../../discussions), if they're turned on for this repository. Otherwise open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose).
- **Something's broken:** open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose) with the bug form.
- **A security problem:** don't post it in public. Report it privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What to include

- The Broski version: Settings › About on the Mac or on the iPhone.
- Your macOS or iOS version, and whether it's the Mac app or the iPhone app.
- The model provider the problem involves, if any (Claude, OpenAI, Ollama, …).
- What you did, what happened, and what you expected.
- The last lines of the event log. On the Mac, Settings › About › **Copy diagnostics for feedback** copies your version, settings and the log's last lines; the log is also at `<Broski folder>/.broski/broski.log`.

The log never holds what you said, but it does hold file paths, which can include your user name or a project's name. Remove anything personal before you paste it. Never paste API keys, notes or conversation text.

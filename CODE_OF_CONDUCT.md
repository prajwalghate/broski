# Code of conduct

Broski is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone who takes part (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) agrees to this.

**Do:**

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be specific: what you saw, what you expected, what you tried.
- Make room for newcomers; explain instead of dismissing.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

**Don't:**

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately.
- Use sexual language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances.
- Publish anyone's private information, including notes or data from their Broski folder.
- Keep going after someone has asked you to stop.

**If something goes wrong,** contact the maintainers privately through the repository's Security tab (Report a vulnerability) or by a direct message to a maintainer. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block people who break these rules, starting with a warning where that's enough.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
